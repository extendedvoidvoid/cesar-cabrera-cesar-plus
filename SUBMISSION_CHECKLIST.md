## 🎯 **How to Submit - Step by Step**

### Step 1: Prepare Documents (1-2 days)
1. Review and finalize `STATION_F_APPLICATION_EN.md`
2. Update `CraftCut_Pitch_Deck.pptx` with latest traction
3. Test demo at https://extendedvoidvoid.github.io/atelier-synesthesie/
4. Ensure GitHub repo is up to date (`git push origin main`)

### Step 2: Request Recommendations (2-3 weeks before submission)
1. Identify 1-2 recommenders (former employers, clients, partners)
2. Send them `LETTER_OF_RECOMMENDATION_TEMPLATE.md`
3. Provide them with:
   - Application document
   - Demo link
   - Your resume/CV
   - Key achievements to highlight

### Step 3: Fill Online Form (1 day)
1. Go to: https://stationf.co/founders-program
2. Create account / Log in
3. Use content from `STATION_F_APPLICATION_EN.md` Section 13 (Quick Answers)
4. For long-form questions, copy from relevant sections:
   - "What does your startup do?" → Section 3
   - "What's your innovation?" → Section 4
   - "Why you?" → Section 7 (Team)
   - "Why Station F?" → Section 11

### Step 4: Upload Supporting Documents
1. Pitch deck (PDF format)
2. Optional: Letter of recommendation (PDF)
3. Optional: Additional portfolio materials

### Step 5: Verify & Submit
1. Review all form fields for completeness
2. Check that all links work
3. Verify contact information is correct
4. Click "Submit"
5. Save submission confirmation email
