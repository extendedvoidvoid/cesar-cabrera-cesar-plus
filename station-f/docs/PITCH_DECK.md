## 1. Executive Summary / Résumé Exécutif

### 🇬🇧 English
**CraftCut** is an **AI video automation platform** that finally works for **demanding brands** — those bound by a high-spec creative brief in motion design, typography, and editorial rhythm. Developed in Paris, our engine synthesizes the best of motion design: beat-sync pacing, total subtitle control, mobile safe-zones, organic grain — industrialized for 9:16.

Our **entry point** is **pop culture and album covers** — a natural vector to engage artists and labels, before expanding into **youth-targeted podcasts** in phase 2. In parallel, CraftCut forms a **complementary duo** with real **weekly reportages** — around architecture and *Les Ouvriers de France* — and feeds **daily short videos** that **develop** key information and brand messages to **build audience loyalty** every day.

For international reach, we offer **translation and voice cloning** across all European languages. When brands refuse to **dilute their official stats**, we deploy **fan or satellite pages** that support the brand's message, heritage, and news — creatively and daily — without cannibalizing the main account.

The pipeline is **validated and cloud-deployment ready**. **350 localized vertical videos per day. 64.3 million LLM tokens per month.**

## 3. Product Promise (Power Words — IP-Protected)

**We synthesized the best of motion design** — kinetic rhythm, Swiss typographic compositions, beat-sync transitions, organic grain — and encapsulated it in an automation engine that **enforces the creative brief** on every output.

**Total subtitle control** — alignment, glyphs, safe-zones, zero UI collision — guarantees broadcast readability on mobile where competitors fail.

**Editorial duo:**
- **Weekly reportages** (field, architecture, Ouvriers de France, brand behind-the-scenes) — depth and credibility.
- **Daily development videos** — short formats that deepen a message, news item, or brand detail to build loyalty.

**Smart internationalization:**
- **Translation + voice cloning** across all European languages.
- **Fan / satellite pages** when brands refuse to dilute official stats — creative, daily support for the brand narrative.

**Cloud-deployment ready** — multi-agent orchestration, parallel rendering, horizontal scale on enterprise infrastructure (Google Cloud Run, Azure AKS, AWS Fargate).

**Volume opérationnel cible :** 50 campagnes/jour × 7 langues = **350 vidéos verticales/jour** · **64,3 millions de tokens LLM/mois**.
