## 3. What CraftCut Does

CraftCut is the **first AI video automation platform that premium brands can actually trust** — built by a Netflix media veteran and the creator of the world's first AI-recreated literary voices.

While competitors produce "AI slop" — generic, repetitive content with poor typography and inconsistent motion — CraftCut **enforces broadcast standards** through a proprietary multi-agent pipeline that extends my **Artist Academy Paris fine-tuning methodology** from literature to video:

- **Beat-sync motion design** — Visual transitions perfectly aligned with audio rhythm
- **Swiss typography control** — Collision-free subtitles, safe zones for mobile, custom glyph rendering
- **Multi-language satellite pages** — Clone voice in 7+ European languages without diluting main account stats
- **Organic film grain** — Premium aesthetic that mimics professional cinematography

**Vertical focus:** 9:16 format optimized for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts

## 4. Problem We Solve

In 2026, three critical gaps prevent premium brands from adopting AI video:

1. **Quality Ceiling:** AI generators cannot meet luxury brand standards for motion design, typography, and visual consistency
2. **Daily Engagement Gap:** No structured layer between weekly long-form content and social media posts
3. **International Fear:** Brands avoid multi-language publishing on main accounts due to stats dilution

CraftCut solves all three: **premium-quality automation**, daily short-form development videos, and **satellite page strategy** for international markets.
